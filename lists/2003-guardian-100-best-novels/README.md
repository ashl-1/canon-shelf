# »The 100 Greatest Novels of All Time« (The Observer)

This list of the »100 greatest novels of all time«, including non-English novels, was compiled by Robert McCrum for »The Observer« in 2003, »with a lot of help from colleagues«. »The Observer« was acquired by Guardian Media Group Limited in 1993 and operated as a sister paper to »The Guardian«. McCrum's list was published on Sunday, 12 October 2003. Here is [the first snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20031205051417/http://observer.guardian.co.uk/review/story/0,6903,1061037,00.html) in the Internet Archive.

# Links
- [The 100 greatest novels of all time: The list](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2003/oct/12/features.fiction) (which served as basis for our listification)
- [Introduction](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2003/oct/12/fiction.shopping) by Robert McCrum

We transformed the list into a machine-readable list and enriched it with metadata on authors and works (Author Wikidata ID, Author GND ID, Work Wikidata ID, Work GND ID, Work Goodreads ID).

There are just minor differences between the first snapshot of the list saved in the Internet Archive and the current version on The Guardian's website. These corrections primarily involve special characters and spaces in first-name abbreviations. In one instance, a typo in a preposition was corrected; in another, a title was updated:

- 12. Honore De Balzac → Honoré De Balzac
- 17. Emily Bronte → Emily Brontë
- 18. Charlotte Bronte → Charlotte Brontë
- 32. Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde → The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde
- 47. E. M. Forster → EM Forster
- 63. E. B. White → EB White
- 70. Gunter Grass → Günter Grass
- 76. Gabriel Garcia Marquez → Gabriel García Márquez
- 78. John Le Carre → John Le Carré
- 83. V. S. Naipaul → VS Naipaul
- 84. J.M. Coetzee → JM Coetzee
- 94. Haroun and the Sea af Stories → Haroun and the Sea of Stories
- 95. La Confidential → LA Confidential
