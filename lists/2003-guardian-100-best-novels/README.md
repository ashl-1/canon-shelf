# »The 100 Greatest Novels of All Time« (The Observer)

This canon of the »100 greatest novels of all time«, including non-English novels, was compiled by Robert McCrum for »The Observer« in 2003, »with a lot of help from colleagues«. »The Observer« was acquired by Guardian Media Group Limited in 1993 and operated as a sister paper to »The Guardian«. McCrum's list was published on Sunday, 12 October 2003, here is [the first snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20031205051417/http://observer.guardian.co.uk/review/story/0,6903,1061037,00.html) in the Internet Archive.

# Links
