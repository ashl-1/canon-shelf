# Joachim Kaiser: »Kaisers Klassik. 100 Meisterwerke der Musik«
*(= »Kaiser's Classics: 100 Masterpieces of Music«)*

This canon, compiled by the German musicologist, critic, and journalist [Joachim Kaiser](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joachim_Kaiser) (1928–2017), comprises 100 works of classical music. It was originally published in book form in 1997 and republished in 2017. The present digitised list is based on the 2017 edition. The [table of contents](https://d-nb.info/1133031927/04) of that edition was digitised by the German National Library.

We converted the canon into a machine-readable list and enriched it with metadata relating to composers and works (Composer Wikidata ID, Composer GND ID, Work Wikidata ID, Work GND ID).
