# »Readers’ Top 100 Novels of All Time« (The Guardian)

Three weeks after publishing its list of the ["100 best novels of all time"](https://www.theguardian.com/books/ng-interactive/2026/may/12/the-100-best-novels-of-all-time), compiled from the votes of 172 literary experts, the Guardian published a companion list: "Readers’ top 100 novels of all time". This second list was based on votes from "more than 3,000 readers".

Readers were invited to vote for up to three novels via a form published on the Guardian's website on 12 May 2026 ([earliest snapshot in the Internet Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20260512174100/https://www.theguardian.com/books/2026/may/12/tell-us-what-are-your-top-three-novels-of-all-time)), the same day the countdown to the full expert list began. The readers' list was published on 6 June 2026.

The methodology behind the ranking is unclear. Many positions are shared; for example, 13 novels are listed at rank 80. Given the large number of voters, it seems unlikely that so many works received exactly the same number of votes.

We transformed the list into a machine-readable dataset and enriched it with metadata on authors and works (Author Wikidata ID, Author GND ID, Work Wikidata ID, Work Goodreads ID, Work GND ID).
